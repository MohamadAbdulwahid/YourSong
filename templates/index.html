About YourSong

YourSong is an innovative application that creates personalized music tracks based on your favorite parts of songs. Here's how it works:

1. Upload or Link

Upload your audio file or provide a link to your favorite song from platforms like YouTube, Spotify, or Apple Music.

2. Select Favorite Part

Use our intuitive interface to select your favorite part of the song that you listen to the most.

3. Get Your Custom Track

Our AI analyzes related songs and creates a seamless custom track with similar musical parts.
